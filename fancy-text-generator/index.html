Cool fonts you can paste anywhere

These are not fonts. Each style rewrites your letters as different Unicode characters (mathematical bold, script, fraktur, double-struck, circled letters and so on). Because they are real characters, they survive copy and paste into places that never let you pick a font: Instagram bios and captions, TikTok, X and Facebook names, Discord usernames, WhatsApp, YouTube comments, plain text files.

Most modern phones and browsers render all 27 styles. A few (squared, negative circled) depend on the reader's device fonts, so double-check anything important. Screen readers may spell some styles letter by letter; use them for decoration, not whole paragraphs.

Why does the text still paste as fancy letters?

Because they are Unicode characters, not styling. The look travels with the text, which is exactly why it works in bios and usernames where formatting is stripped.

Will these work in my Instagram bio?

Yes. Copy a style, open your profile, edit bio, paste. Instagram accepts Unicode in bios, captions, comments and most usernames.

Numbers or symbols did not change. Why?

Some Unicode alphabets only exist for A-Z letters. Anything without a fancy equivalent passes through unchanged.
